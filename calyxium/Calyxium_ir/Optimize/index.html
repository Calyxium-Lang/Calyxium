Module Calyxium_ir.Optimize

val optimize : Ir.instr list -> Ir.instr list

optimize instrs performs IR-level optimizations over the given list of instructions and returns an optimized instruction list.

The exact optimizations may include constant folding, dead-code elimination, expression simplification, and other IR transformations depending on the current optimizer implementation.

  • parameter instrs

    The list of IR instructions to optimize.

  • returns

    A new list of IR instructions with optimizations applied.
